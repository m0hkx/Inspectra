# Screenshots

A quick tour of Inspectra, seen as an admin, with two sites, six assets and three months of inspection history.

[Overview](#overview) · [Inspections](#inspections) · [Issues](#issues) · [Work orders](#work-orders) · [Assets](#assets) · [Sites](#sites) · [Templates](#templates) · [Schedules](#schedules) · [Members](#members)

---

### Overview

The answer to *"What needs attention?"* Alerts for overdue inspections, unassigned issues and fixes awaiting verification, a 90-day inspection activity chart, the week ahead, and the logbook.

<img src="./images/screens/01-overview.png" alt="Overview with needs-attention alerts, inspection activity chart, inspections due, open issues by severity, work orders by status, next inspections and the logbook" width="100%" />

### Inspections

Checklists created from schedules, split into *Due*, *Overdue* and *Submitted*. Due times are shown in each site's local time.

<img src="./images/screens/02-inspections.png" alt="Inspections table with Due, Overdue and Submitted tabs, showing inspection number, asset, inspector, due time and status" width="100%" />

### Issues

Every failed checklist item opens an issue. Admins turn open issues into work orders with one click.

<img src="./images/screens/03-issues.png" alt="Issues table with status and severity filters, showing the failed item, asset, severity, status and linked work order" width="100%" />

### Work orders

Every fix in progress, as a board by status. Closed work orders, verified or cancelled, are listed below.

<img src="./images/screens/04-work-orders.png" alt="Work orders board with Open, In progress, On hold and Awaiting verification columns, above a table of closed work orders" width="100%" />

### Assets

The equipment your team inspects, where each piece lives, and how many open issues it has.

<img src="./images/screens/05-assets.png" alt="Assets table with site and status filters, showing category, site, location, open issues and status" width="100%" />

### Sites

The places your equipment lives. Each site has its own timezone, and inspections are scheduled in it.

<img src="./images/screens/06-sites.png" alt="Sites table with name, address, timezone and asset count" width="100%" />

### Templates

The checklists inspectors fill in. Each item has a default severity for when it fails. Edits only apply to future inspections.

<img src="./images/screens/07-templates.png" alt="Template editor with a list of templates and an ordered checklist with a severity for each item" width="100%" />

### Schedules

Which checklist runs on which asset, how often, at what local time, and for which inspector. The next inspection is shown in local time and in UTC.

<img src="./images/screens/08-schedules.png" alt="Schedules table with checklist, asset, repeat rule, inspector and next inspection, plus pause and delete actions" width="100%" />

### Members

Invite people and set their role. Each role says what it can do.

<img src="./images/screens/09-members.png" alt="Members page with an invite form and a table of members with their role" width="100%" />
